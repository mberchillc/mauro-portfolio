About

A humanistic approach to technology reporting.

Mauro Berchi is a bilingual journalist and communications strategist based in Miami. For more than a decade, he has investigated, used, taught and built around the technologies reshaping communication — from digital media and social platforms to AI, Web3 and AI-driven software.

Available for reporting, interviews and editorial collaborations.
